## What happened

The @openodia YouTube channel went live, dedicated to making AI and NLP education accessible to Odia speakers worldwide. Content covers large language models, speech recognition, natural language processing, and practical Odia AI tooling — delivered in both Odia and English.

## Why it matters

Education is the bottleneck for Odia AI adoption. Most AI tutorials assume English proficiency and access to English-language datasets. @openodia bridges this gap by teaching core AI concepts in Odia and demonstrating them on Odia language tasks.

## Featured content

The channel features beginner-friendly introductions, advanced NLP tutorials, conference talks, and community spotlights — all focused on making Odia a first-class language in AI.
