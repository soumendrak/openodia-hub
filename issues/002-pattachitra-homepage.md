## What to build

Replace the production homepage presentation with the approved Pattachitra composition while preserving its live ecosystem counts, contributor data, community videos, structured data, and working routes. The hero presents the exact Odia `ଓ` at monumental scale inside the painted panel, with both peacocks visible throughout the subtle rotation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The homepage ships the palm-leaf ochre page ground, painted hero panel, large gold `ଓ`, decorative border, collection, community, and learning sections from the approved design.
- [ ] The phrase is rendered exactly as `ଆମ ଭାଷା ଆମ ପରିଚୟ`, with no trailing bar or danda.
- [ ] The hero letter's slow rotation and separate light effects pause, resume, and respect reduced motion; the painted panel itself remains fixed.
- [ ] The letter has clear space above the lower ornament so both peacocks remain visible at every point in its animation.
- [ ] Existing live stat loading, community-video data, FAQ JSON-LD, breadcrumb JSON-LD, and directory links are retained or replaced with equivalent user-visible behaviour.
- [ ] The homepage is usable at 320px, 390px, tablet, and desktop widths.
