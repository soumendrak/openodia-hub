## What to build

Redesign the production Tutorials route as the learning chapter of the Pattachitra experience. Keep its server-rendered video feed, search, channel sections, playlists, video schema, and direct YouTube destinations. Give GDG Cloud Bhubaneswar a first-class channel section using the verified channel registration already added to the repository.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The Tutorials route has the approved ochre, vermilion, gold, and decorative hierarchy, plus a focused learning hero and channel grid.
- [ ] GDG Cloud Bhubaneswar appears in introductory attribution and the channel feed when videos are available, linking to `https://www.youtube.com/@gdgcloudbbsr`.
- [ ] Existing server rendering, search shortcut, video cards, playlists, loading fallback, and VideoList JSON-LD stay functional.
- [ ] The associated GDG community page is available from a visible community path without representing the chapter as operated by OpenOdia.
- [ ] The route remains readable and fully navigable at 320px, 390px, tablet, and desktop widths.
